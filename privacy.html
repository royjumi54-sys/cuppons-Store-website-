Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information Cuppons Store collects through the Telegram bot @TheCupponsStoreBot and this website, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Telegram details: your Telegram ID, username and first name.
  • Wallet and order data: balance, deposits, order history and delivered items.
  • Payment references: the Razorpay payment ID and amount for each deposit.
  • Referral data: who invited you, if you joined through a referral link.

We do not collect or store your UPI PIN, bank account details or card numbers. These are handled by your UPI app, your bank and Razorpay.

How we use it

  • To run your wallet, process your orders and deliver products.
  • To confirm payments and prevent fraud or duplicate credits.
  • To answer support requests and send order and payment messages.
  • To send announcements through the bot. You can stop by blocking the bot.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay processes your payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Telegram carries the messages between you and the bot.
  • Our product suppliers receive the order details needed to fulfil your purchase, not your payment details.
  • Our database provider stores the data above on our behalf.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep account and order records for as long as your account is active and as needed for accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your data by contacting us. We may need to keep some records for legal reasons.

Security

Access to our systems is restricted and data is sent over encrypted connections. No online service is completely risk-free, so keep your Telegram account secure.

Contact

For privacy requests, see Contact Us.
