How it works

  1. Open the bot

    Start a chat with @TheCupponsStoreBot on Telegram. No sign-up form.

  2. Add funds

    Choose an amount in rupees and pay the UPI QR with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app.

  3. Pick a product

    Browse the live catalog. Prices and stock are shown before you confirm.

  4. Get it instantly

    Your item arrives in the chat and stays in My Orders.

Clear payments

Deposits are made in Indian rupees by UPI. Your wallet shows the balance in US dollars at the rate displayed in the bot before you pay. Every deposit and order has a reference number you can quote to support.

We never see your UPI PIN or bank details. Payments are handled by Razorpay.
