Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Cuppons Store sells digital products that are delivered instantly. This policy explains when an order is replaced or refunded.

Cancelling an order

An order can only be cancelled before you tap Confirm Purchase. After confirmation, delivery is immediate and the order cannot be cancelled.

Faulty or invalid products

  • If a delivered item does not work as described, report it within 24 hours of delivery with your order ID.
  • After we verify the problem, we will replace the item or add the order amount back to your wallet.
  • We cannot refund items that were already used, changed or shared, or problems caused by not following the product's instructions.

Failed orders

If an order fails, the amount is returned to your wallet automatically. If the order shows as "under review", we check it manually and either deliver it or restore your balance.

Payment problems

  • Money debited but wallet not credited: wait a few minutes, then use Check Payment in the bot. If it still has not been added, contact us with your payment reference. Verified payments are credited manually, or reversed to your bank by Razorpay.
  • Duplicate or wrong-amount payment: we refund the extra amount to the original payment method.
  • Payment to an expired QR: contact us with the payment details and we will help resolve it.

Wallet balance

Wallet balance is meant to be spent in the store. An unused deposit can be refunded to the original payment method on request within 7 days of the deposit, if none of that money has been spent.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds to the original payment method are started within 3 business days. Your bank usually takes 5 to 7 business days to show the money.

How to ask

Contact us through the details on the Contact Us page and include your Telegram ID and order or payment reference.
